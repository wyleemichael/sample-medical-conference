Welcome

A meeting built around the next clinic, not the last slide deck.

HKIMC 2027 brings oncology, cardiology, infectious disease, surgery, endocrinology, and primary care into one harbour-front programme. The aim is practical: what to start, stop, or measure when you go home.

Join colleagues from across Asia-Pacific and beyond for named plenaries, small-group workshops, and a tightly edited abstract programme.

“If a session cannot change a ward round, a consent conversation, or a referral pathway, it does not belong on this programme.”

Prof. Elena Vargas, Conference Chair
