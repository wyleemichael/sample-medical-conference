Abstracts

Call for abstracts.

Deadline: 15 January 2027, 23:59 HKT. Presenting authors must register.

Guidelines

  • Maximum 300 words, structured as Background, Methods, Results, Conclusion where possible.
  • One figure or table may be described in text; files are not uploaded in this demo.
  • Case reports need consent language and no identifiable data.
  • Encore abstracts are allowed if disclosed.
  • Notifications by 5 February 2027.

Presentation types

Oral (12 minutes), moderated poster, and exhibition poster. The abstracts chair assigns format after scoring.
